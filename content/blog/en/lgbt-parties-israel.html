Tel Aviv is one of the best-known gay-friendly cities in the world, and its LGBTQ+ nightlife is rich and varied: big parties, small bars, drag shows and community nights. Outside Tel Aviv, the scene keeps growing too.

What the scene offers

  • Big parties: regular club nights with DJs and a dance-till-morning vibe. Pop and house dominate, but there's techno and more too.
  • Drag shows: evenings of live performance, humor and plenty of sparkle. Great even if you're not up for dancing all night.
  • Bars and community nights: a more relaxed atmosphere and a good place to meet people.
  • Parties for women and diverse crowds: there are regular nights and events aimed at different parts of the community.

Pride Month

June is the peak of the year: Pride parades in Tel Aviv and other cities, and a whole week of parties, outdoor events and shows. Tickets for the big Pride Week parties sell out early, so start watching a few weeks ahead.

  • Timing: the big parties start late. Arrive before midnight and you'll find a half-empty dance floor.
