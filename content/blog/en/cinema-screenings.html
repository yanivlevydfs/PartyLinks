Going to the movies isn't just about catching the latest release at the mall. Some screenings are a real event: a classic film on the big screen, a screening with the director in the room, or a movie under the stars on a summer night.

Where to find special screenings

  • Cinematheques: Several Israeli cities have a cinematheque with a changing program, themed film series and evenings with a talk before the movie.
  • Screenings with a Q&A: A conversation with the director, actors or critics after the film. Book ahead, since seats are limited.
  • Outdoor cinema: In summer there are screenings in parks, on rooftops and on beaches, sometimes for free.
  • Screenings in bars and cultural venues: Small movie nights, usually with a more social atmosphere.

Film festivals

Film festivals run throughout the year in Israel: general, documentary, student film, and festivals focused on a theme or country. At a festival you see films that won't always reach regular distribution, and you often get to meet the filmmakers too.

  • Plan ahead. Festival programs are published a few weeks in advance, and the most popular screenings sell out fast.
  • Multi-film passes: Many festivals offer a pass for several films, which pays off if you plan to see more than one.

Enjoy evenings with substance? There are also talks and lectures.
