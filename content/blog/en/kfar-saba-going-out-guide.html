Kfar Saba knows exactly what it is: not clubs until morning, but a good cultural evening close to home. The city's main stage, the Culture Hall, hosts something almost every night, and the variety is bigger than many people expect.

The Culture Hall

Kfar Saba Culture Hall is the city's main stage. It hosts productions by Israel's major theatres, musicals, children's shows during the holidays, and the "Hot Jazz" series, which brings jazz nights in a range of styles, from New Orleans to New York.

Stand-up, sing-alongs and Beita

Stand-up by well-known comedians comes to the Culture Hall too, almost every week, and so do the "Bimat HaZemer" sing-along nights (a popular Israeli tradition of group singing). For a smaller, more intimate evening there's Beita, the Raizel culture and arts center, with plays by small theatre companies, premiere screenings with a talk by the director, and workshops.

For all stand-up nights across Israel there's a stand-up page that updates constantly, and for jazz lovers there's also a jazz page.
