Kfar Saba knows exactly what it is: not clubs until morning, but a good cultural evening close to home. The city's two main venues, the Culture Hall and the Municipal Auditorium, host something almost every night, and the variety is bigger than many people expect.

The Culture Hall

Kfar Saba Culture Hall is the city's main stage. It hosts productions by Israel's major theatres, musicals, children's shows during the holidays, and the "Hot Jazz" series, which brings jazz nights in a range of styles, from New Orleans to New York.

The Auditorium: stand-up and sing-alongs

The Municipal Auditorium is the go-to venue for stand-up by well-known comedians, sing-along nights (a popular Israeli tradition of group singing), lectures and chamber concerts. It's a smaller stage with the audience close up, which makes a big difference at a stand-up night.
