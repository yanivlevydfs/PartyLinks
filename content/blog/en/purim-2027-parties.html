Purim is the one holiday of the year when everyone dresses up in costume, which makes it one of the best nights of the year to go out in Israel. In 2027 the holiday falls on Tuesday, March 23, with the Megillah reading on the evening of Monday, March 22. As usual, Jerusalem celebrates a day later, on Shushan Purim, Wednesday, March 24.

How to plan Purim 2027

When the holiday falls midweek, the celebrations stretch over several nights. Here is how it looks this year:

  • Thursday to Saturday night, March 18–20: the weekend before the holiday. Many of the big costume parties happen here, and this is when tickets sell out first.
  • Monday evening, March 22: Purim night. After the Megillah reading, bars and clubs fill up, and some parties go on until morning.
  • Tuesday, March 23: the holiday itself. Many areas have street parties and daytime events, and Holon traditionally hosts the Adloyada, its big Purim street parade.
  • Wednesday, March 24: Shushan Purim in Jerusalem, one more night of celebrations for anyone who wants another round.

Which party is right for you

Purim parties come in almost every style. If you want to dance to the hits, you'll find most of the parties under mainstream. Fans of the 90s and 2000s should go for the throwback parties, and the electronic scene has techno and house parties with a very relaxed dress code.

Purim tips

  • Buy early. Purim parties are among the most in-demand events of the year, and the first ticket tier is the cheapest. More tips in our guide to saving on tickets.
  • Pick a costume you can dance in. Heavy masks and bulky costumes look great in photos, less so after three hours on the dance floor. For ideas, take a look at our Halloween guide too.
  • Check the age limit. The minimum age is listed on every event, and on Purim most parties are 18+ or 21+.
  • Plan your ride home. Demand for taxis is very high on Purim night. Sort out in advance who you're going home with and how.
