Greek music has a large and loyal following in Israel. The sound of the bouzouki, the familiar songs and the warm atmosphere make for an evening that is half concert, half celebration. It's a great night out for friends, couples and families, and for anyone who loves to sing along at the top of their lungs.

Types of Greek nights

  • A singer or band in concert: a full musical evening of classic Greek songs and hits, sometimes in Hebrew versions too.
  • Taverna-style night: food, drinks and live music at your table. The mood starts relaxed and heats up as the night goes on.
  • Greek party: a dance floor, a DJ and Greek hits mixed with Mediterranean music. This is where people dance.

Tips

  • Book ahead. At nights with table seating, spots sell out fast, especially on weekends.
  • Check what's included. At some nights the ticket includes a meal or drinks. The details are on the event page.
  • Bring a group. A Greek night is most fun when you have people to sing and dance with.
