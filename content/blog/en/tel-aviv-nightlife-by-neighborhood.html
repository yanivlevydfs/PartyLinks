Tel Aviv is a small city with a big nightlife. Most spots are a short ride from each other, yet every area has its own character. Knowing the map helps you choose better: not just which party, but where to start the night and where to head next.

South Tel Aviv: the big clubs

The old industrial and workshop districts of south Tel Aviv are home to the club scene. Big buildings, high ceilings and few neighbors make room for powerful sound systems and parties that end in the morning. This is where you'll find most of the techno and house parties and the big weekend events. Nearby neighborhoods like Florentin are packed with small bars that are perfect for the start of the night.

Rothschild and Lilienblum: the city-center bars

The city center is all about bars, restaurants that turn into bars at night, and DJ nights. The vibe is mixed: some places are quiet enough for conversation, while at others people are dancing on the bar by midnight. It's the easiest area to kick off a night out, since everything is within walking distance.

Jaffa: a different atmosphere

Jaffa offers a mix of bars with sea views, live music venues and art spaces. It's especially good if you want a less crowded night, with good food beforehand and maybe a small show afterwards.

The Port and the beach

The north of the city, around the Tel Aviv Port, is an area of large venues, mainstream parties and events that draw a broad crowd. In summer, beach bars host sunset sessions and parties that start early. This is the area for big groups and for anyone who wants to dance to the hits.

Tips for going out in Tel Aviv

  • Start late. At clubs the dance floor usually fills up after midnight. Before that, it's bar time.
  • Free early entry. Many places offer free entry until a certain hour, so arriving early saves money. We cover these events in our guide to saving on tickets.
  • Get home safely. Public transport is limited on weekends because of Shabbat (Friday evening to Saturday evening). Plan a taxi or shared ride in advance.
