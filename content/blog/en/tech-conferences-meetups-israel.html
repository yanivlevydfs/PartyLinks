Israel's tech industry meets a lot outside the office. There's a meetup, talk or networking night almost every evening, and conferences throughout the year draw hundreds or thousands of people. You'll find them all here, straight from the platforms where they're published.

Meetups and networking nights

Meetups are the easy way to meet people in your field. It's usually an evening at a host company, with a talk or two, food and time to chat. They suit job seekers, people who want to learn a new technology and anyone who just wants to stay up to date.

For every tech event, head to our tech page, and the big conferences have their own conferences page.

Startups and entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurs and founders will find pitch nights, workshops, investor meetups and community events here. They're good places to get feedback on an idea, find co-founders or hear how others built a company.

The full list is on our startups and entrepreneurship page.
