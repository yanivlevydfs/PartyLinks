Ashdod is a large city on the Mediterranean coast with a cultural life that doesn't always get the attention it deserves. Its events calendar is broad: concerts, museums, talks and workshops, plus plenty of community events in its neighborhoods.

The Performing Arts Center

The Ashdod Performing Arts Center (HaMishkan) is the busiest stage in the city. Most of Ashdod's plays, musicals and big concerts take place there, alongside talk nights such as "Café Da'at". It's the first place to check when you're looking for a cultural evening in the city.

Shows and music

Dyona Hall is another main stage, with concerts and plays, including quite a few shows in Russian for the city's large Russian-speaking community. Stand-up nights are mostly at Beit Yad LaBanim. Ashdod is also home to the Israeli Andalusian Orchestra, which blends Andalusian and Moroccan music with Israeli compositions and performs at culture halls across the country.

All shows by Israeli artists are on the Israeli music page, and if you're after Mizrahi music (Israeli pop rooted in Middle Eastern and North African styles), you'll find it on the Mizrahi page.

The Ashdod Art Museum

The Ashdod Art Museum, at the MonArt Center, shows rotating exhibitions, and its workshop wing, "Techniques", runs art workshops throughout the week.

The Museum of Philistine Culture

The Museum of Philistine Culture is the only museum of its kind in Israel, with a permanent exhibition and rotating shows. It's a good option for the afternoon, before an evening out in the city.
