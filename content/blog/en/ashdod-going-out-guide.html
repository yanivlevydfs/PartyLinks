Ashdod is a large city on the Mediterranean coast with a cultural life that doesn't always get the attention it deserves. Its events calendar is broad: concerts, museums, talks and workshops, plus plenty of community events in its neighborhoods.

MonArt and the Ashdod Art Museum

The MonArt Center is the city's cultural hub. It houses the Ashdod Art Museum, with rotating exhibitions and workshops, along with halls that host talks, small performances and music nights.

Shows and music

Dyona Hall is one of the city's main stages, with concerts by Israeli singers, bands and stand-up comedy shows. Ashdod is also home to the Israeli Andalusian Orchestra, which blends Andalusian and Moroccan music with Israeli compositions.

All shows by Israeli artists are on the Israeli music page, and if you're after Mizrahi music (Israeli pop rooted in Middle Eastern and North African styles), you'll find it on the Mizrahi page.

The Museum of Philistine Culture

The Museum of Philistine Culture is the only museum of its kind in Israel, with a permanent exhibition and rotating shows. It's a good option for the afternoon, before an evening out in the city.
