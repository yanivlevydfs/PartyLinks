Jerusalem surprises people who come only for the history. It has a dense bar scene, live music venues, art-house cinema and plenty of cultural events, and in most cases they're all within walking distance of each other.

Mahane Yehuda Market at night

By day it's a market, and in the evening the stalls close and the bars open. The market at night is one of the liveliest places in the city: music, food and tables between the alleys. From here it's easy to continue to the city center, where pubs and bars stay open late.

The First Station and the Cinematheque

The First Station, set in the historic railway station, hosts concerts, markets and open events, and suits a more relaxed evening. Nearby is the Jerusalem Cinematheque, with screenings, film festivals and meetings with filmmakers, plus a view of the Old City walls.
