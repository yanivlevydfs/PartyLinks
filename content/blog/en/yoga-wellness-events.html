Not every night out has to be a party. A yoga workshop, a meditation evening or a weekend retreat is another way to make time for yourself, meet people and leave calmer than when you arrived.

What's on offer

  • Drop-in yoga classes: one-off classes, sometimes outdoors in a park or on the beach. No membership needed.
  • Workshops: breathwork, meditation, movement or a specific yoga practice. Usually a few hours, with close guidance.
  • Sound healing: a relaxation evening with singing bowls and gongs. No experience or physical effort required.
  • Retreats: one or several days away from the city, with practice, food and quiet. Check in advance what the price includes.
  • Free dance and ecstatic dance: dancing with no alcohol and no steps, just moving to the music.

Tips

  • What to bring: a mat, water and comfortable clothes. For sound healing, also a light blanket, since you lie still the whole time.
  • Arrive early. A few minutes to settle in and unwind before you start makes a difference.
  • Health limitations: if you have an injury or a medical condition, tell the instructor before class.
  • First retreat? Start with a single day before committing to several.
