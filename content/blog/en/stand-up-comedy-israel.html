A stand-up comedy night is one of the safest nights out there is: great for couples, friends and first dates, no dancing until dawn required, and everyone leaves in a good mood. But not every stand-up night is the same.

Types of nights

  • A comedian's show: an hour or more of prepared, polished material. Pricier, so buy in advance.
  • Comedy night: several comedians, each with a short set. A good way to get to know new names.
  • New material night: a comedian tries out new jokes. Not everything lands, and that's part of the fun. Usually cheaper.
  • Open mic: up-and-coming comedians take the stage. Sometimes very funny, sometimes less so, and always cheap.

Tips

  • Front row? Only if you're ready to become part of the show. Comedians love talking to whoever sits close.
  • Phones away. Most shows ask you not to film. The material isn't ready for the internet yet.
  • Arrive on time. At many venues seating isn't assigned, and whoever arrives first gets to choose.
  • Age. Some shows are for adults only. The minimum age is listed on the event page.
