Artificial intelligence is the busiest field in Israel's tech scene right now, and the events calendar shows it. Every week there are meetups, talks, hackathons and conferences on language models, AI agents, computer vision and practical uses of AI.

For developers and researchers

Technical meetups are where you hear from the people actually building AI products: how to run models in production, how to evaluate them and which tools really work. Many take place in the evening at company offices, with advance registration and no charge.

For anyone who wants to understand AI

You don't need to write code to get something out of an AI event. There are beginner talks, workshops on using AI tools in everyday work, and panels on how the technology is changing business, education and culture. They're a great place to start.
