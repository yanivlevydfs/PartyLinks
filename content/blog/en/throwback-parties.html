Few moments on the dance floor beat a song everyone knows and sings out loud. That's why throwback parties work so well: you don't need to know any DJs or genres, just show up and sing. The only question is which decade.

The decades, in brief

  • The 80s: synth-pop, new wave and huge dance hits. A colorful, happy vibe.
  • The 90s: Eurodance, pop, hip hop and R&B. A decade packed with high-energy hits.
  • The 2000s: pop, R&B and club hits everyone remembers from high school or the army.
  • Israeli hits: many parties also include a block of Israeli songs from the same decades.

Upcoming throwback parties

All the parties are on the throwback page, and there's also a page for throwback parties this weekend. Into old-school grooves? Try the disco and funk parties too.

Tips

  • Check the decade. An 80s party and a 2000s party are very different experiences. The decade is in the party name or description.
  • Arrive a bit early. At throwback parties the dance floor usually fills up earlier than at electronic parties.
