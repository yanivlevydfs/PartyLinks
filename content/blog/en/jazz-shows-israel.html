Live jazz is one of the most exciting nights out there is. Every night is different from the last, because much of the music is created on the spot. Israel has an active jazz scene with excellent local musicians, small clubs, concert halls and festivals.

Types of jazz nights

  • Jazz club: a small stage, tables up close and an intimate atmosphere. Perfect for a date or a relaxed evening with friends.
  • Concert hall show: a larger ensemble or a well-known artist, with proper seating and good acoustics. Worth buying in advance.
  • Jam session: musicians improvising together. Less predictable, and that's exactly what makes it fun.
  • Jazz with influences: fusion, Latin jazz, soul and groove. A good way into the genre through familiar sounds.

Tips

  • Arrive on time. At small clubs seating is usually unassigned, and the good tables go fast.
  • Listen, don't talk. At jazz shows the audience stays quiet while the band plays. Applaud after a solo, it's part of the tradition.
  • Check the lineup. A trio, a quartet or a big band are very different experiences. The ensemble is listed on the event page.
