A good lecture is one of the most satisfying nights out there is: a relaxed evening, an interesting topic, and you leave with something new in your head. The range in Israel is huge, from history and science to food, travel and psychology.

Types of lectures

  • Single lecture: one evening, one topic. A good way to try out a new speaker or subject without committing.
  • Lecture series: several sessions on the same topic. Ideal if you want to go deeper, and usually better value than single tickets.
  • Talk or panel evening: several speakers, an open conversation and audience questions. Less structured and more lively.
  • Lecture with music or a screening: evenings that combine content with live music, a film or a rich visual presentation.

Tips

  • Read the summary. It tells you whether the lecture is for a general audience or for people who already know the field.
  • Timing. Many lectures take place in the morning or early evening, not just at night.
  • Language: there are also lectures in English, Russian and other languages. The language is usually listed on the event page.
  • Free: libraries and museums are a good source of open lectures. More ideas in the guide to free entry events.
