Disco and funk parties have some of the happiest dance floors around. The rhythm is warm, the bass is bouncy and the vibe is less serious than at a classic electronic party. It's a great choice if you love to dance but aren't looking for a hardcore night until sunrise.

What you'll hear on the dance floor

  • Classic disco: The big hits of the '70s and '80s, strings and powerhouse vocals.
  • Funk and soul: Bass, horns and a groove you can't stand still to.
  • New disco and nu-disco: Fresh productions with an old-school spirit, often close to house music.
  • Edits: New versions of old songs, reworked for today's dance floor.

Tips

  • Dress for fun. Sequins and color are perfect for a disco floor, even when there's no dress code.
  • Check the DJ. Some DJs play mostly classics and others prefer new productions. The lineup is on the event page.
  • Great for groups. A disco party is a good pick for a birthday or a night out with friends who aren't all electronic music fans.
