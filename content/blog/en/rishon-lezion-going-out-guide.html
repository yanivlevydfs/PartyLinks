Rishon LeZion is one of Israel's largest cities, and its cultural calendar is just as big. Most of what happens in the city in the evening is concentrated on two stages, the Culture Hall and the municipal auditorium, and the range is wide: from classical music to an Elton John tribute.

The Culture Hall

The Rishon LeZion Culture Hall is one of the busiest venues in central Israel. It hosts symphony concerts throughout the season, productions by the major theater companies, dance troupes from Israel and abroad, and tribute shows to artists such as Frank Sinatra and Elton John. During Hanukkah it fills up with children's plays.

All the concerts in Israel are collected on our classical and opera page, and the tribute shows on the throwback page.

The auditorium and Hebrew sing-along nights

The municipal auditorium hosts lectures, Hebrew sing-along evenings and musical gatherings centered on Israeli songwriters. It's the place for a quieter night out, usually at a lower price too.

LAGO and big events

The LAGO complex in the city hosts conferences and large events, including tech events and the occasional party. It's worth checking the list, because what's on there changes a lot from month to month.
