Summer in Israel is long, hot and packed with events. Long evenings, the school summer break and predictable weather make it the season of outdoor concerts, beach parties and festivals. The dates of most festivals for summer 2027 haven't been announced yet, but you can already plan around the calendar.

Summer 2027 calendar

  • Shavuot, Thursday to Friday, June 10–11: a long weekend that kicks off the season. Beach parties, events on kibbutzim and the first outdoor parties.
  • The long summer break, July and August: peak season for outdoor concerts, festivals and beach parties.
  • Tisha B'Av, Wednesday evening to Thursday, August 11–12: a Jewish day of mourning and fasting. Most venues are closed or quiet, so don't plan a night out for this one.
  • Tu B'Av, Wednesday, August 18: Israel's festival of love, with lots of parties and shows on the evening before and the weekend closest to it.

Festival types worth knowing

Israel's festival scene is diverse, and new events are added every year. These are the main types:

  • Electronic festivals and outdoor parties. Nature and beach parties, mostly trance and techno, that sometimes run for two days or more.
  • Music and city festivals. Such as Hutzot Hayotzer in Jerusalem and the Klezmer Festival in Safed, which traditionally take place in summer and combine concerts with a street-fair atmosphere.
  • Jazz. The Red Sea Jazz Festival in Eilat was held at the end of summer for many years. You can see upcoming shows on our jazz page.
  • Big outdoor concerts. In summer, amphitheaters and parks host tours by Israeli and international artists.

Check the exact dates on each festival's official website, because they change from year to year, and sometimes the location does too.

Festival season tips

  • Buy in the first tier. At festivals, the difference between the first and last tier can be big. More tips in our guide to saving on tickets.
  • Water, shade and a hat. An outdoor event in the Israeli summer takes preparation, even at night. Most festivals publish in advance what you're allowed to bring in.
  • Plan your transport. Many festivals take place far from the city and run organized shuttles. Book them together with your ticket.
