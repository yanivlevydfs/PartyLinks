Latin music is one of the happiest things you can hear on a dance floor. In Israel it lives in two worlds: reggaeton and Latin hits parties with a DJ, and partner dance nights such as salsa and bachata. Both are fun, but they are very different.

The styles in brief

  • Reggaeton: a bouncy dembow beat, Spanish-language hits and a packed dance floor. You dance on your own or with friends.
  • Latin pop: well-known Latin hits, usually played alongside reggaeton at the same party.
  • Salsa: a fast, energetic partner dance with steps and spins.
  • Bachata: a slower, closer partner dance. Considered beginner-friendly.

Tips

  • The opening class is worth it. Even if you come alone you'll find people to dance with, because partners rotate at many nights.
  • Comfortable shoes. There's a lot of dancing at salsa and bachata nights, and shoes with smooth soles help with the spins.
  • Reggaeton parties fill up late. Like most club nights, the peak comes after midnight.
  • Respect the dance floor. At partner dance nights, ask politely, and take a "no" with a smile.
