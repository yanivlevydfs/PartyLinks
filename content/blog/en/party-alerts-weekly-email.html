Dozens of new events go up on ticketing sites every day, and the party you were waiting for can sell out before you even see it. Instead of checking every day, save what you're looking for once and let SPOTX tell you.

An alert is a saved search

Pick what you like on the home page: techno in Tel Aviv at the weekend, jazz in Haifa, everything with free entry, or one artist. Press "Save as alert", and from then on every new event that matches shows in the bell at the top of the site and in the "For you" row on the home page. An alert finds exactly what the same filters show on the site, so there are no surprises.

You can also save an alert from the "Where to?" questions on the home page, and every event page has a button for an alert on its artist.

A bell on an event

Saw a party and haven't decided yet? Tap the bell on the card and it goes to "Saved". If you don't have an account yet, a short sign-in with Google opens, and the event is saved as soon as you're back. Your saved events wait for you on every device and go into the weekly email.

A weekly email

Every Thursday morning, ahead of the weekend, one email: your saved events for the coming week, what's new for your alerts, and what's on this weekend with free entry. Each event comes with a picture, time, place, weather and price. It only goes to people who asked for it on their account page, and one click stops it.

No sign-up required

The whole site is open without signing up, and that won't change. An account is just an extra: one click with Google, no new password, and you can delete it any time. What is stored and why is in the privacy policy.

What's on this weekend

Want to keep it? Open this weekend's events, pick a genre or area, and press "Save as alert". It's all on your account: My alerts.
