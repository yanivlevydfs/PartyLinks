When it comes to culture, Ra'anana is one of the busiest cities in the Sharon region north of Tel Aviv. Its events calendar is full almost every evening, and most of it is local and community-focused: concerts, lectures, stand-up, screenings and family activities. If you want a big party you'll head to Tel Aviv, but for a good night out close to home you don't need to leave the city.

Yad Lebanim Culture Hall

The Culture Hall is the city's main stage. It runs regular series such as sing-along evenings with comedians, music lectures and advance film screenings, alongside concerts by Israeli artists and stand-up shows.

Ra'anana Music and Arts Center

The Music Center hosts intimate acoustic shows by well-known artists, musical evenings and chamber concerts in its small salon. The hall is smaller than the Culture Hall, and that is exactly its advantage for a quiet show.

All the shows by Israeli artists across the country are collected on our Israeli music page, and there's a stand-up comedy page too.

Ahuza Street

Ahuza Street is the city's main drag, lined with cafes, restaurants and bars. It's the place for dinner before a show or a drink after it.
