Eilat, Israel's resort city on the Red Sea, is built for vacations, and its nightlife follows suit. In the evening the city moves to the promenade, the hotels and the beach, and throughout the year it hosts festivals that bring in crowds from all over the country.

The promenade

The beachfront promenade is where every evening in Eilat begins. Bars, restaurants and stalls, music coming from every direction and lots of people out and about. Even if you're not planning a long night, you'll find something to do here after sunset.

Shows and parties at the hotels

A large share of Eilat's shows take place at hotels, in their halls and on their stages. There are Israeli singers, stand-up comedy and parties, and they're usually open to people who aren't staying at the hotel. For all shows by Israeli artists, there's the Israeli music page.

Festivals

Eilat is one of Israel's festival cities, with music, sports and culture events that stretch over several days. If you're planning a vacation, it's worth checking what's on during your dates, because a festival can turn an ordinary weekend into a completely different experience.
