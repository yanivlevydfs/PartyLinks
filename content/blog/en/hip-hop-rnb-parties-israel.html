Hip hop and R&B are among the easiest genres to love on a dance floor. Everyone knows the songs, the beat makes you want to move, and the vibe is social and fun. The scene has a big following in Israel, and hip hop and R&B parties take place in Tel Aviv and many other cities, on weekends and midweek too. In this guide you'll find what to expect, how to pick a night and what to know before you head out.

What do they play at a hip hop party?

A typical hip hop party moves between a few worlds. There are the rap classics, songs the whole floor knows by heart. There's current trap, with heavy bass and slow, hard-hitting beats. And many DJs also mix in dancehall, Afrobeats and reggaeton, because they work great on the same floor. The result is a varied night where the energy rises and falls but hardly ever stops.

Some parties focus on a particular era, for example nights of 90s and 2000s hits, while others play only what's hot right now. The event description and lineup will tell you where the night is headed. There are also parties that mix Hebrew rap with the international hits, which is worth checking out if you like the local scene.

So what's an R&B night?

R&B is the soft, sensual side of the scene. More singing, more melody, a slower groove and an intimate atmosphere. A good R&B night usually blends classics from the years when the genre ruled the charts with current artists, and it's a great night for couples as well as a group that wants to dance without jumping all night.

In practice, the line between hip hop and R&B parties is thin. Many parties mix the two styles, for example opening with R&B and gradually building up to hip hop and trap as the night goes on.

You'll find all R&B parties on the R&B page.

When should you arrive?

As with most club parties, the dance floor usually fills up after midnight, and the peak comes later. But there's a good reason to arrive early: many hip hop parties offer free entry or a reduced price until a certain time. On SPOTX that time is listed on every event, and you can read more about it in our guide to free-entry events.

Ticket tiers matter too. Popular parties sell tickets in stages, and the first tier is the cheapest. If you know you're going, buy early. More tips in our guide how to save on party tickets.

What to wear?

The hip hop scene has a strong connection to fashion, and people tend to put effort into their look. That doesn't mean you need designer labels, but it's worth showing up well dressed. Some venues enforce a dress code, and some are completely relaxed. If there's a dress code, it's usually in the event description. When in doubt, it's better to make a bit more effort than a bit less. And either way, wear shoes you can comfortably dance in.

A few dance floor rules

  • Come as a group, but don't block the floor. A circle of friends in the middle of the dance floor is fun, just leave others room to pass and dance.
  • Respect personal space. A crowded dance floor is not an invitation to touch anyone who didn't ask. If someone isn't interested, move on.
  • Drink water. A packed floor heats up fast, and a long night takes more water than you'd think.
  • Get home safely. Plan in advance how you're getting home, especially if you've been drinking.

How to choose a party

  • By the DJ. Every DJ brings their own style. Listen to one of their sets beforehand and you'll know if the night is for you. On SPOTX every artist has a page with all their upcoming gigs, and you can browse the artists page.
  • By the musical style. Classics, trap, R&B or a mix. The event description usually tells you.
  • By age. Many parties have a minimum age, listed in the event details. Bring ID.
  • By area. The map shows what's happening near you, and the Tel Aviv page shows everything going on in the city.

Related genres worth knowing

If you love hip hop and R&B, there's a good chance you'll also enjoy reggaeton and Latin parties and Afro parties, which share a lot of the same rhythm and energy. And if you're after a night of hits everyone knows, you'll feel at home at mainstream parties too.
