Netanya is a beach city with an advantage most cities don't have: a promenade on a cliff above the sea that works any evening of the year. Around it is a city with a very diverse crowd, from longtime residents to immigrants from France and English-speaking countries, and an events calendar that reflects that.

The Municipal Cultural Hall

Heichal HaTarbut Netanya, the city's Cultural Hall, is its main stage. It runs regular series of Israeli song nights, nostalgia shows and movie songs, plays by theatre companies from all over the country, dance and ballet performances, and Las Vegas-style international shows. Children's shows are added during the holidays.

Looking for more comedy nights? The stand-up page gathers every show in Israel, and the nostalgia page has the tribute shows and old hits.

Netanya Museum

The Netanya Museum offers guided visits and activities that suit a different, calmer kind of evening, or the afternoon hours before a show.

The promenade and Independence Square

Independence Square (Kikar HaAtzmaut) and the streets around it are the city center, with cafes and restaurants open until late. From there you head down to the promenade, which in summer is one of the nicest places in town to end an evening.
