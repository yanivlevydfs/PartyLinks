Haifa is a city of neighborhoods, and each one offers a different kind of night out. There are bars and parties, concerts, theatre and cinema, and the distances are short enough to combine a few of them in the same night.

Downtown (Ir Tachtit)

The area by the port has been revitalized in recent years and is now the place to go out at night. Bars, clubs and parties are concentrated in its old streets, and on weekends the area fills up. If you're after electronic music, check the techno page.

Hadar

Hadar is Haifa's alternative neighborhood: small bars, galleries, music nights and a diverse local culture scene. It's the place to discover new artists and enjoy a less planned evening.

The German Colony

The German Colony's main boulevard, with its view up to the Bahá'í Gardens, is a great starting point for an evening. Restaurants, cafes and bars line the street, and in summer lots of people stay out until late.
