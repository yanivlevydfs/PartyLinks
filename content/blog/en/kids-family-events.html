Finding something to do with the kids that doesn't end in another day in front of a screen is a constant challenge, especially during school holidays. The good news: there are far more children's shows, workshops and family activities than you might think.

What's on for kids

  • Children's theatre: well-known plays, shows starring favorite characters and original productions. They usually run about an hour, which is just right for little ones.
  • Music shows: concerts for kids and sing-along shows.
  • Workshops: arts and crafts, science, cooking. Great for kids who need to do things, not just sit and watch.
  • Museums and outdoor activities: many museums offer guided family activities, mainly on weekends and holidays.

Upcoming family events

All kids' events, with filters by date and region, are on the kids and family page. Looking for something on Saturday? Head to kids' events this weekend.

School holiday tips

  • Book early. During Hanukkah, Passover and the long summer break, children's shows fill up fast.
  • Pick the right time. For young kids, a morning or midday show beats one that clashes with nap time.
  • Budget. A whole family means a lot of tickets. It's worth looking for free events too, and plenty of them are for kids.
  • Parking and access. If you're bringing a stroller, check ahead whether there's an elevator and parking nearby.
