The cost of getting into a party isn't a fixed number. The same night at the same venue can cost completely different amounts depending on when you bought, which site you used and what time you arrived. Here's everything you need to know to pay less.

1. Free entry before a set time

This is the simplest way to save. Many bars and clubs in Israel offer free entry to people who arrive early, for example until 23:30 or until midnight. The venue gets a dance floor that fills up early, and you save the whole entry price. Watch the time: free entry until 23:30 doesn't apply at 23:31. We show the exact time on every event, and the label updates once it passes.

2. First tier

Most parties sell tickets in tiers: the first is cheap, and each tier that sells out pushes the price up. The difference between the first and last tier can be big, especially for large parties and holidays. If you know you're going, buy early. Every event page shows all the tiers, what's sold out and what's still on sale.

3. Service fees

The price you see at first isn't always what you pay. Ticketing sites add service fees, and they differ from site to site. That's why we show both the service fee and the total price for every ticket, so you can compare the real price.

4. Same event, several sites

It's common for the same party to be sold on several ticketing sites at once, or through the pages of different promoters, at different prices. SPOTX merges all the listings for the same event and shows you the cheapest price among them. On the event page you'll find every option for buying.

5. Midweek

Parties from Sunday to Wednesday are usually cheaper than on Thursday and Friday (the Israeli weekend), and sometimes have no entry fee at all. If your schedule is flexible, check what's on tonight.

6. When tickets sell out

An event marked sold out doesn't always stay that way. Sometimes extra tickets are released, and sometimes a new tier opens. We scan the sites all the time and flag events that are back on sale.

The bottom line

  • Arrive early when there's free entry.
  • Buy in the first tier when you know you're going.
  • Compare the final price, including service fees.
  • Check whether the event is sold on several sites.
