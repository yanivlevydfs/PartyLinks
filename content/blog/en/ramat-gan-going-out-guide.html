Ramat Gan sits right next to Tel Aviv, so it's easy to forget it has stages of its own. In fact, the city has several small, active theaters, regular stand-up nights and a rich community events calendar. It's a good place to start your evening, and if you want to carry on into the night, Tel Aviv is just minutes away.

The city's theaters

Ramat Gan Theater on Hibat Zion Street, the Russell Theater and the Diamond Theater host live shows, stand-up, Saturday culture evenings and children's plays. These are relatively small stages where you get to see the performers up close.

Stand-up at Bleecker

Bleecker Bakery in Ramat Gan hosts stand-up nights featuring stars of the Comedy Bar, in a format with several comedians in one evening. It's a great way to discover new comedians, usually for less than a single-artist show.
