Israel has a special bond with trance, and above all with psytrance. The local scene is big and long-established, and it lives both in city clubs and at nature parties that go until sunrise and beyond. Here's how to pick the party that's right for you.

The styles, in brief

  • Psytrance (full-on): fast, energetic and rolling. The sound of most nature parties.
  • Progressive: slower and groovier, with gradual build-ups. A comfortable entry point for newcomers.
  • Dark and forest: fast, dark and experimental. Mostly played in the late hours of the night.
  • Melodic trance: big melodies and emotional peaks, closer to the EDM world.

Nature parties or clubs

A nature party is a full night outdoors: a dance floor under the open sky, a diverse crowd and an open, free-spirited vibe. It takes a bit of planning around getting there, what to wear and when to sleep. A club night is shorter and easier, with reliable sound and a trip home the same night. In summer and the in-between seasons there are more outdoor parties, while in winter the scene moves mostly into clubs.

Tips for a long night

  • Drink water constantly. At outdoor parties the morning sun can catch you off guard, and you drink more than you think.
  • Plan your way home. Check shuttles in advance, pick a designated driver, or find somewhere to rest before driving.
  • Earplugs. Sound systems at nature parties are loud, and your ears need to last until morning.
  • Leave no trace. Whatever you brought, you take with you.
