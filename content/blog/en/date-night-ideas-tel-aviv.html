Bar, dinner, then another bar. It works, but after a few times it starts to feel like autopilot. Tel Aviv is full of events that turn an ordinary date into a night to remember: small gigs, jazz nights, stand-up comedy shows and intimate parties. This guide offers ideas by type of date, and explains what works for a first date and what's better saved for couples who already know each other.

First date: what should you choose?

A first date has two goals that pull against each other a little: do something interesting, while still leaving room to talk. So it's best to avoid a loud party where you can't hear a word, and an event so long there's no graceful way out.

  • Short and well-defined. A show of an hour or an hour and a half is the perfect length. If the date is going well, you move on to a bar. If not, the evening ends politely.
  • Gives you something to talk about. After a gig or a show there's always something to discuss, which takes the pressure off.
  • Earlier in the evening. An event that starts at 8 or 9 pm leaves time to sit down afterwards, without getting home at 3 am.

Stand-up comedy: shared laughter breaks the ice

A stand-up comedy show might be the safest date idea there is. You laugh together, there's no need to fill silences, and by the end of the show you already have inside jokes. Most comedy clubs serve drinks and light food, so you can combine it with dinner.

One important tip: if you don't want the comedian asking you in front of the whole audience "So how did you two meet?", don't sit in the front row. And another: showcase nights with several comedians back to back are a good choice when you don't yet know the other person's sense of humor. Jazz bar: the classic date

A jazz night is practically the definition of a romantic date. Dim lighting, live music that doesn't drown out the conversation, and a good drink. Unlike a big concert, at a jazz bar you can talk quietly between numbers, move closer to the stage when something grabs you, or just lean back and listen.

Many jazz nights happen midweek, which makes them a great choice for a Tuesday or Wednesday date, when the city is quieter. And if you're not sure you're a "jazz person": you don't need to know any theory to enjoy great musicians up close.

All upcoming jazz nights are on the jazz page.

Live music: when you want something with energy

A band or a singer at a small club makes for a date with more energy. It works especially well when you know you share a taste in music, or when you want to discover a new artist together. Gigs at small clubs feel intimate, without the crowds of a big concert. We cover every kind of show, from the small club to the amphitheater, in our guide to live music in Israel.

Just keep in mind: at a loud, standing-room show it's hard to talk. It's perfect for a third or fourth date, less so for a first meeting.

A small party: for couples who love to dance

A party doesn't have to mean a huge club until morning. There are DJ nights at bars, small parties in intimate spaces, and disco and funk nights where the vibe is happy rather than aggressive. These are places where you can dance together and also step outside to talk.

For couples who already know each other, a party in a new genre is a great way to break the routine. If you've never been to a house party, for example, our guide to house music parties can help you choose.

Early-evening events

Not every date has to start at 10 pm. Some events start early, like sunset parties, early-evening gigs, early stand-up shows and weekend afternoon events. They suit a first date, anyone with an early start the next day, and couples with kids who need to get back to the babysitter at a reasonable hour. Long-term couples: how to refresh date night

  • Take turns choosing. One time one of you picks the event, the next time the other, with no veto. That's how you discover new things.
  • Go back to something familiar. A show by the artist you listened to on your first date, or a night in the same style. Every artist's page in the artists list shows all their upcoming shows.
  • Build a full evening. Dinner, a show and a bar, all in the same area. The map helps you find an event near a restaurant you've already picked, and our guide to Tel Aviv nightlife by neighborhood helps you choose an area.
