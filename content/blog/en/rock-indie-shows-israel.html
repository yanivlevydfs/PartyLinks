Israel's rock and indie scene lives mostly on small stages: bars, live music clubs and independent spaces. Alongside the big concerts in large venues, there's a local band playing to a small, passionate crowd almost every night. Here's how to find your night.

Types of shows

  • Band night: several bands on one night, each with a short set. Perfect for discovering new names.
  • Club show: one artist with a full set, a crowd close to the stage and high energy.
  • Big concert: a hall or amphitheater, full production and songs everyone sings along to. Buy in advance.
  • Acoustic and intimate: guitar, voice and a quiet audience. Very different from a loud rock show, and just as moving.

Tips

  • Doors open is not showtime. Many venues have an opening act, and the headliner goes on later.
  • Earplugs. The sound at small clubs is especially loud, and good earplugs don't spoil the experience.
  • Standing or seated? Some shows are standing only. This detail is listed on the event page.
  • Support the artists. Independent artists rely on ticket and merch sales, and every ticket helps.
