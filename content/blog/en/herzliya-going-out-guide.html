Herzliya doesn't try to compete with Tel Aviv for parties that end at sunrise. Its strength is different: a rich lineup of shows, comfortable venues, and a short drive from anywhere in the Sharon region. A good night out in Herzliya is usually a concert or a play, followed by a drink by the sea.

Herzliya Performing Arts Center

Most of the city's events take place at the Herzliya Performing Arts Center. Throughout the season it hosts a concert series, dance performances, productions by Israel's major theatres, tribute shows to artists like Elvis, sing-along nights and stand-up comedy. It's the place to check when you want a well-organized cultural evening, with an assigned seat and parking nearby.

Zappa, the Cinematheque and smaller stages

Zappa Herzliya hosts shows in a more intimate format, where you sit at a table close to the stage. Herzliya Cinematheque offers screenings and film events, and the community centers, such as Noga Center and HaMesila Center, host stand-up shows and small concerts.

The marina and Herzliya Pituach

If you want to combine dinner or a bar, you'll find most places in the marina and Herzliya Pituach. It's the area to start an evening before a show, or to end a summer night.
