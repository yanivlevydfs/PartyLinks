December is one of the busiest months of the year: Hanukkah brings a whole week of shows for the family, Christmas lights up Haifa and Nazareth, club season is at its peak and New Year's Eve closes the year. There are more than 20,000 events on the calendar this month. Here is what's really planned, straight from the ticketing sites.

Christmas in Haifa and Nazareth

In Haifa, December is the month of the "Holiday of Holidays": Christmas tours in the lower city and Wadi Nisnas, the traditional Scouts' Christmas parade and markets. Nazareth holds concerts, tasting tours and church events all month. Christmas Day falls on Friday, December 25 this year.
