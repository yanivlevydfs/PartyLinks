Beer Sheva is a student city, and Ben-Gurion University sets a lot of its rhythm. Nights here are less packed than in central Israel, but they have a clear character: bars in the Old City, student parties, live shows in small clubs and a big stage that draws audiences from across the south.

The Center for the Performing Arts

The Center for the Performing Arts is the city's busiest stage. It hosts plays by theater companies from around the country, musicals, dance and concerts, and its calendar is full almost every night. For smaller, more experimental theater there is the Fringe Theater Beer Sheva, and the Goodman Acting School stages productions by its students.

The Old City, live music and clubs

Beer Sheva's Old City, with its historic streets, is where the bars and restaurants are concentrated. Live music has two regular homes: Tamuz House of Music, with jazz, Mediterranean music and Israeli singers, and HaHalutz 33, which hosts artists from all over the country. For parties there is the Forum club. What's on shifts with the academic calendar, and the start of a semester is usually the busiest time.

Tech and conferences

In recent years Beer Sheva has become a hub for tech and cybersecurity, and you can see it in the events calendar: conferences, meetups and innovation nights on the university campus and at the Gav-Yam Negev high-tech park next to the train station. All of these are also listed on the tech page.
