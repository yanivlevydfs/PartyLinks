Beer Sheva is a student city, and Ben-Gurion University sets a lot of its rhythm. Nights here are less packed than in central Israel, but they have a clear character: bars in the Old City, student parties, and a theater that draws audiences from across the south.

The Beer Sheva Theater

The Beer Sheva Theater is the city's main stage. It puts on its own productions throughout the season, runs series for young audiences and subscribers, and also offers backstage tours. Many of its productions go on tour around the country, but they look best at home.

The Old City and clubs

Beer Sheva's Old City, with its historic streets, is where the bars and restaurants are concentrated. For parties and late-night shows there are clubs like the Forum. What's on shifts with the academic calendar, and the start of a semester is usually the busiest time.

Tech and conferences

In recent years Beer Sheva has become a hub for tech and cybersecurity, and you can see it in the events calendar: conferences, meetups and innovation nights on the university campus and at the high-tech park next to the train station. All of these are also listed on the tech page.
