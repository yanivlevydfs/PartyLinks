Petah Tikva is one of the largest cities in Israel, but when it comes to going out it usually stays in Tel Aviv's shadow. Those who know it know it has an active stand-up scene, a few good stages and a concert calendar that doesn't require traveling far.

Stand-up: the city's strong suit

Comedy Bar Petah Tikva, which operates at Drink Point on Bareket Street, hosts nights with several comedians in one evening. HaMesila Theatre and Golda Hall bring full-length shows by well-known comedians. If there's one genre where Petah Tikva doesn't need Tel Aviv, this is it.

For every show in the country, head to the stand-up page.

Plays and concerts

For plays by the major theatre companies, musicals and concerts by many singers, people go to the Center for the Performing Arts in Ganei Tikva, right next to the city. It's the big venue in the area, and its calendar stays full throughout the season.
