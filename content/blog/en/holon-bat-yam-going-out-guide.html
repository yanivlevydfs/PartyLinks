Holon and Bat Yam sit right next to Tel Aviv, which makes them easy to overlook. But each has its own events calendar: Holon with theatre, museums and culture for the whole family, and Bat Yam with a beach and promenade that come alive in the evenings.

Holon: theatre and museums

Holon Theatre is the city's main stage, with plays, concerts by Israeli singers and stand-up shows. The Mediatheque complex, which also houses the city library, hosts lectures and cultural events. The Children's Museum and the Design Museum make Holon a good destination during the day too, and if you're coming with kids you'll find events on the kids and family page.

For every event in the city: Holon page.

Bat Yam: promenade and beach

Bat Yam is a beach city, and its evenings center on the sea. The promenade is full of cafes, restaurants and bars, and on summer evenings it's one of the most pleasant spots in the area. It's also a good starting point before a concert or party in nearby Tel Aviv or Jaffa.
